## How to check a notice before you file

1. Find the exact company name in the notice and compare it with the settlement name on the official claim site.
2. Check the date range: most notices only cover purchases, accounts or visits inside a window.
3. Note the claim deadline, then set a reminder a week earlier. Late claims are usually rejected.
4. Keep any confirmation number you get after filing, because it is the easiest way to check status later.
