# Harry Potter Trivia: 10 Questions With Answers and Reasons (study sheet)

Think of this as a short study sheet for Harry Potter. Each item is a question, the correct answer and why it is correct, taken from the full set of 20 on Trivia Nook. The full set lives on the [Harry Potter trivia questions and answers](https://trivia.resuhive.com/books/harry-potter/) page, which lists all 20 questions and lets you play one at a time.

## The questions and answers

**1. In Book 3, whom is Ron's pet rat Scabbers revealed to be?**

Answer: Peter Pettigrew. Scabbers is an Animagus, Peter Pettigrew, who betrayed Harry's parents.

**2. In Book 4 (Goblet of Fire), which three schools compete in the Triwizard Tournament?**

Answer: Hogwarts, Beauxbatons and Durmstrang. The tournament is held at Hogwarts, with Beauxbatons and Durmstrang as guest schools.

**3. In Book 4, who is chosen by the Goblet as the official Hogwarts champion?**

Answer: Cedric Diggory. Harry is added as a fourth champion, while Cedric Diggory is the official Hogwarts champion.

**4. In Book 5 (Order of the Phoenix), who becomes Hogwarts' High Inquisitor?**

Answer: Dolores Umbridge. Dolores Umbridge is sent by the Ministry of Magic and takes control of Hogwarts.

**5. In Book 5, in which Ministry of Magic department are prophecies kept?**

Answer: The Department of Mysteries. The prophecies are stored in the Hall of Prophecy inside the Department of Mysteries.

**6. In Book 7, what are the three Deathly Hallows?**

Answer: The Elder Wand, the Resurrection Stone and the Cloak of Invisibility. The three Hallows come from the Tale of the Three Brothers.

**7. In Book 6 (Half-Blood Prince), which potion does Harry win from Professor Slughorn?**

Answer: Felix Felicis. Felix Felicis is a "liquid luck" potion, awarded for the best potion in class.

**8. In Book 6, who is the Half-Blood Prince?**

Answer: Severus Snape. Snape's mother was Eileen Prince, and he used the name Half-Blood Prince in his old potions book.

**9. In Book 7 (Deathly Hallows), which tale explains the legend of the Hallows?**

Answer: The Tale of the Three Brothers. The story appears in The Tales of Beedle the Bard and is read by Hermione.

**10. In Book 7, who kills Bellatrix Lestrange in the Battle of Hogwarts?**

Answer: Molly Weasley. Molly Weasley duels Bellatrix to protect Ginny and kills her.

## Why explanations matter

A bare answer key only tells you who was right. An explanation tells you why, which is what turns a casual round into something people remember. That is why each answer above carries a short reason, and why the full pages do the same for every one of their questions. Use these as a starting point, then open the full set for the rest.
